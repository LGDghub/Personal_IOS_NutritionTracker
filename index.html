Choose which copy to keep

Your entries save on this device first, then sync while the app is open and online. Your personal ChatGPT connection can retrieve synced records when you ask. It does not receive a message for every tick. Signing out hides your records. Each account has separate device storage. Use separate browser profiles on shared devices.

Import replaces current records after validation and confirmation. Export a backup first. Clearing website data or moving to another URL can separate or remove your saved data.

Your reminders

Reminders use your last cloud sync. Open the app online after logging. Notification text can appear on your lock screen. On iPhone, install to Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).

Connect your ChatGPT

Owner setup is required before the first connection; see the setup guide. In ChatGPT’s custom app/connector settings, add this server using OAuth. Sign in with your own tracker account and choose whether ChatGPT may edit your meals personal targets and Monday weigh-ins, or shopping lists. Availability depends on your ChatGPT account.

Only your account’s records are available. Ask ChatGPT explicitly before it saves changes. You can revoke access below.
