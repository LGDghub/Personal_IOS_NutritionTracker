Choose which copy to keep

Your entries save on this device first, then sync while the app is open and online. ChatGPT can retrieve synced records when you ask in a chat with your Supabase connection. It does not receive a message for every tick. Signing out keeps this device’s records; use your personal device.

Import replaces current records after validation and confirmation. Export a backup first. Clearing website data or moving to another URL can separate or remove your saved data.
