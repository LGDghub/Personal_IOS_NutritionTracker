Backups & sync details

Your entries save on this device first, then sync while the app is open and online. Your personal ChatGPT connection can retrieve synced records when you ask. It does not receive a message for every tick. Signing out hides your records. Each account has separate device storage. Use separate browser profiles on shared devices.

Import replaces current records after validation and confirmation. Export a backup first. Clearing website data or moving to another URL can separate or remove your saved data.

Notifications

A little reminder when you need it

Water reminders show what remains towards your daily target. Supplement reminders let you know when stock reaches the level you choose. Set your hours and frequency below; switch them off anytime.

Reminders use your last cloud sync. Open the app online after logging. Notification text can appear on your lock screen. On iPhone, install to Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).

MADE TO WORK TOGETHER

Get more from Align with ChatGPT

Ask about your logged progress, get summaries and make supported changes to meals, targets and your shopping list through conversation.

You choose what it can read and edit. Align works on its own, too.

1 · Copy your connection link

Name your connection Align by Daly.

https://zrtnabwryzbdqzwghxcp.supabase.co/functions/v1/wfw-mcp
2 · Add it in ChatGPT

Open ChatGPT on the web, preferably on a computer. Go to Plugins → + → Add custom MCP server. Some versions call this Add → Create MCP App.

Enter the name, paste the link under Connection and choose OAuth. Review the warning, then create the connection.

Open ChatGPT Plugins ↗

Available options depend on your ChatGPT account and workspace. If the option is missing, check the current setup guide. This step may not be available in the ChatGPT phone app.

3 · Sign in and choose permissions

When Align opens, sign in using your Align email and password. Choose whether ChatGPT can edit meals, targets and Monday weigh-ins, or shopping. Approve the connection and finish installing it in ChatGPT.

4 · Try your first summary

Start a new chat, select your Align connection (or type @ and choose it), then ask:

Show my logged food, water and weight progress this week. Tell me which days have no records.

Sync Align first. If this connection used to be called WFW, it still works—there is no need to create a duplicate. After granting new permissions, refresh its tools in ChatGPT.
